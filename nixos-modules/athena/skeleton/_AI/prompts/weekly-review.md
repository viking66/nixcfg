Weekly review. Monday morning.

1. Read the last 7 daily notes (`Daily/<dates>.md`).
2. Read `_AI/observations.md` if it exists.
3. Look at `Projects/` — which have had activity this week? Which are stuck?
4. Scan `Inbox/` for items >3 days old; those are stuck too.

Produce a weekly review note at `_AI/reviews/YYYY-WW.md` (ISO week number).
Sections:
- **What got done** — concrete progress on active projects
- **Patterns** — recurring themes, topics Jason mentioned multiple times
- **Stuck** — projects, questions, or inbox items that haven't moved
- **Recommendations** — up to 3 things Jason should consider this week

Commit with `weekly: review YYYY-WW` and push. DM Jason a short version
(the top-level recommendations) via `athena-notify`.
